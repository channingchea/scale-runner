Scale Runner Support

Scale Runner is a piano practice app for drilling scales, chords, and voicings — on a MIDI keyboard or the on-screen keys.

My MIDI keyboard isn’t connecting

  • Scale Runner works with any class-compliant MIDI keyboard over USB or Bluetooth LE.
  • For Bluetooth, open MIDI Devices in the app and pair from there rather than from the iOS Settings app — Bluetooth MIDI devices pair through the app, not the system Bluetooth screen.
  • For USB, check that your adapter passes power to the keyboard. Many keyboards need a powered adapter.
  • Once connected, the app remembers your keyboard and reconnects to it automatically next time.
  • No keyboard? Every mode is fully playable on the on-screen keys.

My timing is being marked late when it feels on-beat

Bluetooth and audio output both add a small delay. Run Settings → Calibrate timing and tap along with eight beats — the app measures the offset for your device and subtracts it from then on. If you switch between a Bluetooth and a USB keyboard, calibrate again.

You can also loosen the timing windows in Settings → Timing difficulty (Easy, Normal, or Strict).

I bought Pro but it isn’t unlocked

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Pro is a one-time purchase and works on all your devices.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Practice with Friends → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, streak, statistics, friendships, and invites. Signing out instead keeps your local practice data on the device.

You can also email us to request deletion.

Do I need an account?

No. Every practice mode works signed out and offline. An account only adds streak backup, friends, applause, and the streak leaderboard.

Practice reminders aren’t arriving

Check that notifications are enabled for Scale Runner in iOS Settings, then confirm the reminder time in Settings → Practice reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they work with no internet connection.
